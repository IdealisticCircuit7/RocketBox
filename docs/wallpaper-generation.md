+OUTPAINT ONLY the attached original close-up rocket wallpaper. Expand the canvas borders slightly, about 6% of original width on each side and 8% of original height above and below, adding seamless continuation of sky, stars, nebula, spaceport structures, clouds and wet platform. The ORIGINAL composition must remain intact in the middle: KEEP the same CLOSE-UP camera distance, the exact large rocket design, position relative to the planet and launch tower, lighting and saturated cyan/purple/gold palette. Do NOT pull camera back. Do NOT turn this into a wide distant establishing shot. Do NOT shrink the rocket or redraw the central scene. The rocket should remain LARGE, roughly 55-60% of the FINAL canvas height, with the nose and wings intact and comfortable safety space at the edges. Preserve detailed foreground and giant ringed planet. Add only modest peripheral scenery as safety bleed for parallax. Final full-bleed landscape around 16:9, no blank borders, no seams, no hard horizontal lines, no rectangles or banding, no text or UI or logo. High resolution. This is a precise border extension of the reference, not a redesign.
